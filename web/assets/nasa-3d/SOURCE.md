NASA 3D Resources are provided by NASA for public use under the NASA media usage guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). The Advanced Crew Escape Suit is the orange launch-and-entry suit (no published walk cycle). The Mark III is a hard-upper-torso planetary surface prototype and is the suit used; ACES is the fallback. Neither file has a skeleton. Both are scaled to 1.80 m and rigged procedurally in `web/hud/astronaut-rig.js`.

The Z2 suit (`3D Models/Z2 Spacesuit`, 16.9 MB) was tried but the download did not finish on 14 Nov 2026; it can be added later.
